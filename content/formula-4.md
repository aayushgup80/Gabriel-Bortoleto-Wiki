# Italian Formula 4 — 2020

Bortoleto made his single-seater debut in the 2020 Italian F4 Championship with **Prema Powerteam**, representing the Brazilian KTF Sports racing academy. [A14 Management](https://a14management.com/driver/gabriel-bortoleto/)

## Highlight

A14 Management describes a strong Mugello weekend with a second place, third place and his first single-seater victory, followed by additional podiums at Monza. This period provided his first full season of car-racing experience.
