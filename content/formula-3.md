# FIA Formula 3 — 2023

Bortoleto joined **Trident** for the 2023 FIA Formula 3 Championship and won the title in his rookie season. The FIA's later announcement of his McLaren development-programme entry confirms that he clinched the championship at Monza and describes his rookie campaign as consistently strong. [FIA Formula 3 announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)

## Historic significance

The FIA described Bortoleto as the **first Brazilian driver to win the FIA Formula 3 title**. He subsequently joined the McLaren Driver Development Programme. [FIA announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)
