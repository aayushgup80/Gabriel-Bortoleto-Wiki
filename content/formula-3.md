# FIA Formula 3 — 2023

Bortoleto joined **Trident** for the 2023 FIA Formula 3 Championship and won the title in his rookie season. The FIA's announcement of his subsequent McLaren development-programme entry describes his rookie campaign as consistently strong and confirms he clinched the championship at Monza. [FIA Formula 3 announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)

## Historic significance

The FIA described Bortoleto as the **first Brazilian driver to win the FIA Formula 3 title**. Winning F3 in a rookie campaign made him a strong candidate for a step up to F2, and he joined the McLaren Driver Development Programme after the season. [FIA announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)
