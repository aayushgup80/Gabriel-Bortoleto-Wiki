# Management and sponsorships

## Management
Bortoleto has been publicly represented by **A14 Management**, a driver management organisation associated with Fernando Alonso. Its profile includes career background and selected early results. [A14 Management](https://a14management.com/driver/gabriel-bortoleto/)

## Reported personal career partners
Secondary biographies have listed associations with Porto Seguro, O Boticário, Banco de Brasília, and Qualcomm/Snapdragon. These are leads for a sponsorship inventory, **not a guarantee each association remains active in October 2026**. Sponsorships change. Verify the current driver website, team partner list and dated announcements before publishing a sponsor list.

## Team-level partners
A team partner is not automatically a personal sponsor. A brand on the Audi car, race suit or team site should not be described as a personal endorsement unless a source explicitly establishes that relationship.
