## Definitions

- **Entries** are not the same as starts; a DNS may count as an entry but not a start.
- **Classified finish** is not necessarily the same as finishing the full race distance.
- **Points** may include sprint points and may be affected by penalties.
- **2026** is in progress: always attach an “as of” date.
