# Formula One — 2025

Bortoleto made his Formula One race debut with **Kick Sauber** in 2025, partnering experienced driver Nico Hülkenberg. Formula 1's profile highlights five top-10 finishes across the rookie season and a difficult home Grand Prix weekend in Brazil. [Formula 1 driver profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)
