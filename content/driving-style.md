# Driving style and racecraft

## Observations supported by results

- **Consistency in F3:** The FIA described his 2023 rookie championship campaign as consistently strong. The season summary reports points in 15 of 18 races. [FIA Formula 3 announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)
- **Racecraft under pressure:** His 2024 F2 Monza Feature Race win from the rear of the field is a concrete example of a strong comeback result. [Formula 1 profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)
- **Adaptation:** Back-to-back F3 and F2 rookie championships demonstrate successful adaptation to two distinct feeder-series levels; this is an inference from results rather than a direct measurement of skill.

## Analysis vs fact

A single comeback cannot prove universal superiority in overtaking, tyre management, wet-weather skill or strategy. Those judgments require multiple races, onboard footage, timing data, conditions and context.

## What remains unavailable

Detailed steering traces, throttle/brake telemetry, tyre temperatures, simulator correlation, engineering debriefs and complete internal radio transcripts are not generally public. This page does not invent such data.

## A reproducible analysis method

When reviewing an onboard or race:
1. Identify track, lap, session and conditions.
2. Link the official timing and classification.
3. Describe visible actions (line choice, braking point, overlap) separately from interpretations.
4. Compare repeated examples before making a trait-level claim.
5. Include counterexamples and the impact of car pace, tyres, DRS and strategy.
