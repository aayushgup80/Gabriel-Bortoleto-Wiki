# Driving style and racecraft

## Results-based observations

- **Consistency in F3:** The FIA described his 2023 rookie championship campaign as consistently strong. Season summaries report points in 15 of 18 races. [FIA Formula 3 announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)
- **Racecraft under pressure:** His 2024 Monza F2 Feature Race win from the rear of the field is a concrete comeback result. [Formula 1 profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)
- **Adaptation:** Back-to-back rookie F3 and F2 championships demonstrate successful adaptation to two feeder-series levels. This is an inference from results, not a direct measurement of skill.

## Analysis vs fact

One comeback does not prove universal superiority in overtaking, tyre management, wet-weather skill or strategy. Such judgments require multiple races, onboard footage, timing data, conditions and context.

## What remains unavailable

Detailed steering traces, throttle/brake telemetry, tyre temperatures, simulator correlation, engineering debriefs and complete internal radio transcripts are not generally public. This page does not invent such data.

## Reproducible analysis method

1. Identify track, lap, session and conditions.
2. Link official timing and classification.
3. Describe visible actions separately from interpretations.
4. Compare repeated examples before making a trait-level claim.
5. Include counterexamples and the effect of car pace, tyres, DRS and strategy.
