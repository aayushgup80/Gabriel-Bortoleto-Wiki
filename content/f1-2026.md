## Current-season data rules

The 2026 championship is ongoing. Do **not** copy a single search snippet or stale career table into a supposedly current record. Points, standings position, race starts, best result and penalties may change weekend to weekend.

### Verify before citing

1. Open [official 2026 driver standings](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/drivers).
2. Check [2026 race results](https://www.formula1.com/en/results/2026/races).
3. Check sprint and Grand Prix classifications separately.
4. Read relevant [FIA event documents](https://www.fia.com/documents) for penalties.
5. Add “as of DD Month 2026” and the retrieval date next to every current-season total.
