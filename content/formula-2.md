# FIA Formula 2 — 2024

Bortoleto progressed to Formula 2 with **Invicta Racing** in 2024 and won the championship in his rookie season. His official Audi biography identifies the back-to-back F3/F2 titles as a rare achievement. [Audi profile](https://www.audif1.com/en/team/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

### Monza Feature Race comeback

Bortoleto won the 2024 Monza Feature Race after starting at the back of the grid, a widely reported comeback and a defining moment of his title campaign. Formula 1's profile specifically highlights his rise from last to first in this race. [Formula 1 profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## Championship context

Winning F2 as a rookie, following his 2023 rookie F3 title, completed consecutive championships in two major FIA feeder series. It helped secure the Sauber F1 opportunity for 2025.
