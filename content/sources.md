## Tier 2 — Secondary reference
- [Wikipedia — Gabriel Bortoleto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto): useful discovery hub; verify major claims through footnotes or official classifications.
- Established motorsport outlets such as Autosport, Motorsport.com, Reuters, AP and reputable broadcasters can support context. Add article-specific links, authors and dates rather than citing only homepages.

## Citation method
- Put direct links beside material claims.
- Record publication date, event date and checked date separately.
- Prefer official classifications and stewards' documents for results/penalties.
- Preserve precise wording for historical records.
- If sources conflict, document both claims and explain which source is primary.
