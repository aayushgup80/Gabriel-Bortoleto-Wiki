- Established motorsport outlets such as Autosport, Motorsport.com, Reuters, AP and reputable national broadcasters can support context and interviews. Add article-specific links, authors and dates rather than citing only homepages.

## Citation method

For every material claim:
- Place the most relevant direct source link beside it.
- Record publication date, event date and checked date separately when different.
- Prefer official classification/stewards' documents for results and penalties.
- Preserve the source's precise wording for historical records.
- If sources conflict, document both claims and explain which is primary.
