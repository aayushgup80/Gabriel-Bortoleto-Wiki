# F1 entry, team pathway and contracts

## McLaren development programme

After winning the 2023 FIA Formula 3 title, Bortoleto joined the McLaren Driver Development Programme. The FIA Formula 3 announcement confirmed the move and quoted programme leadership praising his progress. [FIA Formula 3 announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)

## Sauber race seat

In November 2024, Sauber announced Bortoleto as a driver for the 2025 Formula One season alongside Nico Hülkenberg. Formula 1's profile says McLaren agreed to release him from the development deal to allow the race-seat move. [Audi profile](https://www.audif1.com/en/team/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto) · [Formula 1 profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## Audi transition in 2026

For 2026, Bortoleto entered his second F1 season with the **Audi Revolut F1 Team**, the works operation following Audi's takeover/rebranding of the Sauber entry. The official Audi driver page is the primary source for the current team identity and driver roster. [Audi profile](https://www.audif1.com/en/team/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## Public contract details

A multi-year deal has been publicly described, but this wiki does not reproduce private contract terms that have not been published by the parties. Avoid guessing salary, clauses, or exact expiry dates.
