# Using this wiki for MUN preparation

This subgroup is intended to help use the research responsibly in a brief or speech. Gabriel Bortoleto is a sportsperson, not a state or international organisation; relevance depends on your committee, agenda and assigned role.

## Three levels of material
1. **Biographical background:** nationality, career progression and championships.
2. **Public-interest context:** Brazilian motorsport representation, athlete development, sponsorship and sports commercialisation. Broader claims need independent sources; one driver's experience does not represent all Brazil.
3. **Current facts:** 2026 team, results and incidents. Add an “as of” date and official link to every time-sensitive claim.

## Quick sourced facts
- Bortoleto is a Brazilian driver born in Osasco, São Paulo.
- He won FIA Formula 3 in 2023 and FIA Formula 2 in 2024, both in rookie seasons.
- He joined Sauber for 2025 and drives for Audi in 2026.
- Formula 1 describes him as the first Brazilian full-time F1 driver since Felipe Massa in 2017.

Sources: [Audi profile](https://www.audif1.com/en/team/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto) · [Formula 1 profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## Research etiquette
- Never manufacture quotes or attribute generic statements to Bortoleto.
- Avoid assumptions about political positions or views on international disputes.
- If the committee is not about sport, use this biography only if it supports the agenda.
- Cite original sources with title, publisher, date and URL rather than citing this wiki alone.

Check the committee agenda and rules of procedure before deciding how this biography is relevant.
