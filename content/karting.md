# Karting career (2011–2019)

Bortoleto began karting in October 2011. Consolidated biographies report that he competed in Brazil before moving to Europe at age eleven, with his most successful karting season in 2018. [Career biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto)

## Reported milestones

- **2014:** A14 Management lists him as champion of the Brazilian Karting Open Championship in Cadete, vice-champion in Super Kart Brazil, and third in the Brazilian Karting Championship. [A14 Management](https://a14management.com/driver/gabriel-bortoleto/)
- **2018:** Career summaries report third in both the CIK-FIA World and European OK-Junior championships, plus runner-up finishes in the WSK Super Master Series and Andrea Margutti Trophy. [Career biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto)

## Why karting mattered

Karting gave Bortoleto experience in close racing, qualifying, starts and different circuits before single-seaters. These are general development benefits, not claims about private training methods.
