# Karting career (2011–2019)

Bortoleto began karting in October 2011. Consolidated biographies report that he competed in Brazil before moving to Europe at age eleven, with his most successful karting season coming in 2018. [Career biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto)

## Reported milestones

- **2014:** A14 Management's profile lists him as champion of the Brazilian Karting Open Championship in the Cadete category, vice-champion in Super Kart Brazil, and third in the Brazilian Karting Championship. [A14 Management](https://a14management.com/driver/gabriel-bortoleto/)
- **2018:** Career summaries report third place in both the CIK-FIA World Championship and European Championship in OK-Junior, plus runner-up finishes in the WSK Super Master Series and Andrea Margutti Trophy. [Career biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Bortoleto)

## Why karting mattered

Karting gave Bortoleto experience in close racing, qualifying, race starts, and competing across different circuits before the transition to single-seaters. These are general development benefits, not claims about specific private training methods.
