# Research directory and fact-check status

This dossier has a useful structured starting point, but it should not be described as “every single fact” or a fully audited record yet. Some pages contain summary-level information and verification instructions rather than complete race-by-race data.

## Coverage map

| Driver style | Results-based analysis framework | More onboard examples and race-specific evidence |
| MUN use | Responsible research tips | Adapt to actual committee, agenda and assigned role |

## Minimum evidence standard

For every result, cite the official session classification. For a penalty, cite the stewards' decision. For an interview quote, cite the interview itself and include its publication date. For any current-season total, write “as of [date].”

## Quality labels to use as the research grows

- **Verified primary:** directly supported by official classification, team statement, FIA decision or a direct interview.
- **Verified secondary:** supported by reputable reporting, ideally corroborated by a primary source.
- **Reported / needs checking:** mentioned by a secondary summary but not reconciled against official records.
- **Analysis:** inference from stated evidence, not a directly established fact.
- **Unknown / not public:** avoid guessing.

This page is deliberately transparent about remaining gaps so the wiki can be improved without mistaking summary notes for a complete archive.
