# Records and milestones

## FIA Formula 3 title
Bortoleto won the 2023 FIA Formula 3 drivers' championship with Trident in his rookie season. The FIA described him as the first Brazilian driver to win the F3 title. [FIA announcement](https://www.fiaformula3.com/en/latest/article/gabriel-bortoleto-signs-with-mclaren-driver-development-programme.3w3cxd50uFFxVAFeJPxOW4)

## FIA Formula 2 title
He followed with the 2024 FIA Formula 2 championship, again as a rookie, with Invicta Racing. Audi's official profile highlights the back-to-back F3/F2 feat. [Audi profile](https://www.audif1.com/en/team/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## Monza comeback
The 2024 F2 Monza Feature Race win after starting at the rear is a high-profile comeback. Define the series and precise grid position, and verify official grid/classification before citing a record. [Formula 1 profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## F1 national milestone
Formula 1 describes him as the first Brazilian to compete full-time in F1 since Felipe Massa in 2017. This does not mean no Brazilian participated in any F1 session after 2017; the wording concerns full-time competition. [Formula 1 profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## Avoid record inflation
“Youngest,” “first-ever,” “only” and “unprecedented” require exact historical scope, dated primary sources, and attention to series/format definitions.
