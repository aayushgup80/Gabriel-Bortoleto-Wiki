# Gabriel Bortoleto: overview

**Gabriel Lourenzo Bortoleto Oliveira** (born 14 October 2004) is a Brazilian racing driver from Osasco, São Paulo. By 2026 he is competing in Formula One for the Audi Revolut F1 Team. The official Audi driver profile identifies 2026 as his second F1 season and highlights his back-to-back rookie titles in FIA Formula 3 (2023) and FIA Formula 2 (2024). [Audi driver profile](https://www.audif1.com/en/team/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

## Why he is notable

Bortoleto's path is striking because he won the FIA F3 championship in 2023 and the FIA F2 championship in 2024 during his rookie seasons in both categories, then earned an F1 race seat with Sauber for 2025. Formula 1's profile notes he was the first Brazilian to compete full-time in F1 since Felipe Massa in 2017. [Formula 1 driver profile](https://www.formula1.com/en/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)
