# Peer comparisons

## Narrow, evidence-based comparison
Bortoleto's distinctive recent junior achievement is winning FIA Formula 3 in his rookie season (2023) and Formula 2 in his rookie season (2024). Audi's official profile notes that only a small number of F1 drivers have completed this back-to-back rookie-title route. [Audi profile](https://www.audif1.com/en/team/drivers/gabriel-bortoleto)

This invites comparisons with other feeder-series champions, including Charles Leclerc, George Russell and Oscar Piastri. Calendars, team strength, points systems, competition and race formats differ, however. Junior success does not guarantee future F1 performance.

## Suggested dimensions
| Dimension | Fair comparison method |
|---|---|
| Rookie F3/F2 titles | Verify season and rookie status |
| Wins and podiums | Use official classifications |
| Qualifying | Compare teammate-adjusted results and formats |
| Consistency | Points-scoring rate and variance with race count |
| F1 transition | Consider constructor, reliability, teammate and season |
| Career progression | Use dated facts, not predictions |

## Caution
Do not state “the best rookie ever” as fact. Label it opinion and provide the metric behind it.
